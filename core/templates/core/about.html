Who We Are

A Legacy of Medical Excellence and Innovations

Founded in 1998 with just a single specialty wing, MediCare has grown to become a premium tertiary healthcare hospital. We serve over 50,000 patients annually across multiple campuses, providing advanced diagnostic and surgery procedures.

Our commitment is to bring top-tier healthcare closer to our communities, ensuring safety, empathy, and advanced research are accessible to all. We integrate digital healthcare modules to streamline patient records and appointment schedules.

Our History Timeline

  • 1998
    Founding Year

    MediCare opens as a Cardiology Center with 50 beds.

  • 2008
    Expansion Phase

    Added Pediatrics, Neurology, and Orthopedics. Expanded to 250 beds.

  • 2020
    Digital Health Shift

    Integrated EHR, digital booking portals, and robot-assisted operating rooms.

Our Mission

To deliver compassionate, state-of-the-art healthcare that enhances the life quality of individuals, families, and our global community.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as a health center of excellence, bridging advanced medical research with premium, patient-centric healing environments.

Core Values

Integrity, Empathy, Collaboration, Innovation, and Respect drive every surgery, counseling session, and patient interaction.
