About MediCare Hospital

Learn about our history, mission, advanced infrastructure, certifications, and our dedicated team of medical professionals.

OUR BACKGROUND

Dedicated to Health and Healing Since 2001

Founded over two decades ago, MediCare began as a specialized cardiological care clinic and grew into one of the country's most reputable multi-specialty healthcare networks. Today, we manage over 500 beds, 15 advanced operations theatres, and modern diagnostic laboratories.

We combine international medical expertise with advanced engineering systems to provide surgical treatments, therapeutic interventions, and wellness screening to over 100,000 outpatients annually.

Our Mission

To deliver patient-centric, top-tier clinical services focused on safety, healing accuracy, and ethical medical care.

Our Vision

To become the region's preferred healthcare hub recognized for diagnostic innovation and clinical excellence.

Our Core Values

Integrity, Clinical Excellence, Patient Safety, Compassion, Transparency, and Innovation in all procedures.

INFRASTRUCTURE & TECHNOLOGY

Advanced Medical Engineering Systems

Our clinics host modern medical infrastructure designed to improve recovery speeds and diagnostic accuracy:

  • High-Definition MRI & CT Scans: Precision imaging for neural, cardiac, and musculoskeletal diagnostics.
  • Robotic Operation Rooms: Minimally invasive keyhole surgical facilities for rapid recovery.
  • Automated Pathology: Automated chemical testing lines to deliver lab reports without delays.
